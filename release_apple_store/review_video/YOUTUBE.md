## Description

```
Demo for Apple App Review: the Family To-Do for E-Paper iPhone app working with the open-source e-paper display it was made for.

What the video shows:
1. The e-paper display (ESP32-S3 + 4.2" e-paper), built from the open-source project.
2. Adding a task in the app.
3. The app syncing with the display over Bluetooth Low Energy (no pairing, no internet).
4. The display showing the new task.
5. Marking the task as done, and the display removing it after the next sync.

The hardware is not sold; anyone can build it from the instructions, firmware and 3D-printable case at:
https://github.com/marekpiotrowskimp/family-todo-epaper

The app collects no data: no account, no server. Bluetooth is used only to talk to your own display.
```
