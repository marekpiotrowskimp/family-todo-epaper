## Post

One to-do list for the whole family, always visible on an e-paper display in the hallway. 📋

I built a small open-source display (ESP32-S3 + 4.2" e-paper) and an iPhone app for it, and Family To-Do for E-Paper is now on the App Store.

How it works:
✅ Everyone adds tasks on their own iPhone: priority, due date, reminder
✅ Open the app near the display and it syncs over Bluetooth, with no pairing, no Wi-Fi and no cloud
✅ The display shows what needs doing and whose task it is, and every family member gets at least one row
✅ Completed tasks go to a history with the time they were done
✅ Private notes stay on your phone
✅ Access is protected by a family key shared with a QR code

No account, no ads, no data collection. Available in English and Polish.

Firmware, wiring and a 3D-printable case are on GitHub, so you can build the display yourself.
